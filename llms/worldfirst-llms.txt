## Product Pillars

- Collect Payments
  - Collect business payments from around the world in 20+ currencies for free, as fast and easily as a local.
  
- Make Payouts
  - We've designed the World Account for business, so you can make easy, fast and secure payments to 200+ countries in 100+ currencies.
  
- Foreign Exchange
  - A World Account helps you ensure the better currency exchange, at today's rate, a rate you want to wait for, or one that you've locked in. Your funds, your rules.

- Manage Payments and Accounts
  - Easily track your incoming and outgoing payments, move funds and control your team's permissions from one place. Connect to accounting software to seamlessly reconcile transactions while reducing your admin.

## Who we serve 

- E-commerce sellers/online sellers
  - If you're an online seller, you can benefit from WorldFirst multi-currency account to receive payments from marketplaces and payment gateways, make payouts for business expenses, procurement from 1688 and other partners/vendors, advertising, logistics and more through a selection of payment methods. 

- Importers, exporters and SMEs
